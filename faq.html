Clear answers about caring, learning and keeping your plant memories in PlantSaga.

How do I add a plant and a care routine?

Open Garden and choose Add plant. Identify a plant from a photo or enter its details manually. Open the plant to add care tasks such as watering or feeding, then review your tasks in Today or your care schedule.

What is My Garden?

Garden brings your own plants, their photos, notes and care history together. Group plants by spaces such as Kitchen, Living Room, Balcony or Outdoors.

How do reminders work?

Create a care task and choose its timing in the app. Allow notifications on your device if you want reminder alerts. Notification delivery also depends on your device settings. Check the soil and the plant before watering; a reminder is a prompt to check, not a reason to water automatically.

Can I snooze or skip a task?

Yes. Use the available task actions to move a reminder or skip an occurrence. Review your routine if the timing no longer suits your plant. Snoozing is not a guaranteed automatic AI adjustment to future care.

What happens if I miss a task?

The app keeps care history so you can review completed and missed care. Recurring routines continue according to their schedule; check the app for any overdue care and changes you need to make.

Can I care for a whole room at once?

Premium bulk care lets you select plants in Garden and record a care action for the group. Update to the latest version for available bulk-care controls. Check each plant first: plants in the same room may still need different care.

How do growth journals and Before & After work?

Open a plant’s growth journey to add photos and see changes over time. Before & After lets you compare growth moments and share a graphic. Photo allowances and comparison controls depend on your plan and app version.

What are Discover and the Discovery Wishlist?

Discover helps you explore plants and learn about them. Save plants you are interested in to your Discovery Wishlist. A wishlist is separate from the plants you own in Garden; collection limits depend on your plan.

What is Shared with Me?

Save a plant someone has shared with you so you can return to its shared page. Viewing a shared plant does not give you permission to edit the owner’s plant or change their care tasks.

Can someone view my shared plant without the app?

Yes. A shared plant link opens a webpage for that plant. Friends can view the available shared information there; they can also open PlantSaga when the app and device support it.

What should I check before sharing a plant?

Review the plant details, photos and notes that appear in its shared view before sending a link. Anyone who receives or is forwarded a link may be able to view that shared information. Avoid including personal or sensitive details in plant content you share.

Can AI tell me exactly what is wrong with my plant?

AI provides suggestions and possible explanations, not a guaranteed identification or diagnosis. Check the plant’s soil, light and care history, and seek a qualified local expert when needed.

Does the weather change my schedule automatically?

Weather information can give useful context for plant care. Check your actual growing conditions, soil moisture and existing routine before changing a task. Do not assume that local rain reached an indoor or sheltered plant, or that every schedule adjusts automatically.

How accurate is the Light Lab?

Light measurements are estimates and depend on your device, sensor availability and the measurement method shown in the app. Use them to compare positions and learn about a space, rather than as a replacement for a calibrated horticultural meter.

Should I follow the Moon Guide to decide when to water?

The Moon Guide includes lunar information, gardening traditions and practical care checks. Lunar traditions are not a guarantee of better growth. Prioritise your plant’s condition, soil moisture, light and the season when deciding what to do.

What can I learn from Tools Guide and Daily Insights?

Tools Guide explains equipment, uses and practical choices, with additional utilities for Premium where available. Daily Insights offers short plant-care ideas. Apply advice to your own plant, growing space and local conditions.

Is PlantSaga free to use?

Yes. You can start with Free, which includes a personal Garden, care routines and access to discovery and learning features, within usage limits. Premium adds more capacity and advanced utilities. Check the app for current availability and limits.

How does the introductory Premium offer work?

Eligible accounts can explore an introductory Premium offer in the app. The current model includes a 30-day introductory period and up to three eligible 14-day extensions. Eligibility and extension windows apply; the app shows the current offer and what happens when it ends.

Can I use the same Garden on iOS and Android?

Sign in to the same PlantSaga account to access synced account data on supported devices. Internet access is needed for cloud updates and AI features. Guest use does not provide the same account-based sync.

How can I contact support or suggest a feature?

Use the support or feedback options in Profile, or email team.plantsaga@gmail.com. Include your app version and device model when reporting a problem, and avoid sending passwords or sensitive account details.
