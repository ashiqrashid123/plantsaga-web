A few things you might be wondering

Good questions. Clear answers.

Is PlantSaga free to use?

Yes. You can start with Free, which includes a personal Garden, care routines and access to discovery and learning features, within usage limits. Premium adds more capacity and advanced utilities. Check the app for current availability and limits.

Can I use it for indoor and outdoor plants?

Yes. Organise plants by spaces such as a living room, balcony, patio or garden. Adjust routines to your plant, soil, local climate and season rather than following a fixed rule for every plant.

Can AI tell me exactly what is wrong with my plant?

AI provides suggestions and possible explanations, not a guaranteed identification or diagnosis. Check the plant’s soil, light and care history, and seek a qualified local expert when needed.

Can someone view my shared plant without the app?

Yes. A shared plant link opens a webpage for that plant. Friends can view the available shared information there; they can also open PlantSaga when the app and device support it.
